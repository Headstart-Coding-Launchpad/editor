# Quiz Types Overview

A plain-language summary of the different quiz styles available. No field names or YAML here — for that, see `docs/authoring/quiz-tasks.md`.

## Multiple Choice

Student picks one answer from a list of options. Wrong options can carry their own short explanation of why they're wrong.

## Match

Student drags items to pair them up correctly — for example, matching a term to its definition.

## Fill in the Blank

Student completes a sentence or snippet by dragging the missing word into place, or typing it in.

## Short Answer

Student types a free-text response. It can be auto-checked for a keyword or phrase, or left open for the teacher to review.

## Confidence Rating

Student rates how confident they feel about a topic on a 1–5 scale. There's no right or wrong answer — it's a self-check, not a test.
