# Station activity: one saved space station each child builds with mouse and keyboard skills (Mouse and Keyboard)

- **Status:** planned (Phase 1 on feature/station-activity, Hub #101)
- **Kind:** activity
- **Requested by:** Lesson Gen Agent (approved by Ryan), 2026-10-22
- **Lessons blocked:** none yet

## Need

Mouse and Keyboard (Computer Confidence, ages 7+) is rebuilt around one saved station scene per child (Mouse and Keyboard Skills.md §5, agreed by Ryan 2026-10-22). Each lesson's skill, used for what it really does on a computer, adds a visible part: click presses or selects, double-click opens, drag moves with snap-to-spot, scroll a long catalogue, a right-click options menu, hover name tags, typed nameplates and signs (Shift and Caps Lock tracked), fixing a muddled copy of the child's own text in place, Delete on a selected part, text selection, and Ctrl+C/V/Z/S. Build in phases, each shipping the lessons it covers: Phase 1 Lessons 1-4 (saved scene carried between lessons, click/select, double-click open, drag and snap, Ctrl+Z, scrolling catalogue, unlock-everything-up-to-this-lesson); Phase 2 Lessons 5-6 (right-click menu, hover, small targets); Phase 3 Lessons 7-10 (text on parts, edit-in-place, Delete, selection, copy/paste, Saved state); Phase 4 Lessons 11-12 (station PNG and parent share link). Full spec: §5a.

Closest existing capability (from `lessons capabilities`): Mouse activity (activities/mouse.md): static emoji targets, items done in order, no scene, nothing kept; activities are saved on the device with no carry-through. Keyboard activity edit_text has the edit-not-retype rule the station reuses. Desktop input checks record method but the course no longer uses the Desktop.

Current workaround and why it falls short: Separate Mouse and Keyboard activities each lesson with station-themed emoji targets. It fails the course design's core (§5): nothing the child does adds to a station of their own, so there is no fast big result and nothing to keep.

## Fun moment

Every week the child's own station visibly grows because of the skill they just learned: the lights come on in their colours (L1), crates they open reveal their crew (L2), modules they drag snap into place (L3), one copied solar panel becomes a row in a second (L10). It's saved week to week, and at the Grand Opening (L12) they show it off and a parent link takes it home.

## Checks wanted

Self-marking items like the Mouse activity's, each recording how it was done (the gesture or key used, as the Desktop input checks do; e.g. opened by double-click, not two clicks; capital by Shift or Caps Lock; text edited, not retyped). Outcome per item; itemProgress to the tutor card and session report.

## Devices

Mouse or trackpad and a physical keyboard; touch blocked with a 'needs a mouse' notice. Use the browser's own double-click (the student's own double-click speed). Right-click by two-finger tap and Ctrl+click. Trackpad drags that survive a lifted finger. Cmd on a Mac. Test on a real Mac and Chromebook before launch.
